\# Model Card: AI-ML-DL Concept Tutor



\## Project Overview

An educational application that explains AI, ML and DL concepts

using Generative AI and machine learning models.



\## Models Used

\- Gemini: Text generation and explanations.

\- Sentence Transformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2): Semantic search.

\- BLIP: Image captioning and understanding.

\- Stable Diffusion 1.5: Image generation.

\- gTTS: Text-to-speech conversion.



\## Intended Users

Students and beginners learning AI, ML and DL.



\## Intended Uses

\- Learning technical concepts.

\- Searching educational notes by meaning.

\- Understanding educational images.

\- Generating illustrations and audio summaries.



\## Limitations

\- Gemini may generate incorrect information.

\- BLIP may misinterpret images.

\- Semantic search depends on the indexed documents.

\- Stable Diffusion may generate inaccurate images.

\- The safety demonstration uses basic keyword matching.



\## Responsible AI

Users should verify AI-generated information.

Do not upload confidential or sensitive information.

The application is intended for education, not high-stakes decisions.
